### Introducing Claude Fable 5.1 and Claude Mythos 5.1 Announcements Sep 1, 2026 Our most advanced models for coding and knowledge work. Their research capabilities also offer an early glimpse of how AI models will contribute to scientific progress.

### Claude Fable 5 and Claude Mythos 5 Announcements Jun 9, 2026 Our next generation of intelligence for the hardest knowledge work and coding problems.
