### Introducing Claude Sonnet 5.5 Announcements Sep 28, 2026 A clear upgrade over Sonnet 5 that runs 30% faster and costs up to 30% less for most work.

### Anthropic releases Sonnet 5.5, which it calls a significantly cheaper, faster work partner
